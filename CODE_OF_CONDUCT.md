# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who participates in the Tangent Browser project — filing issues, submitting pull
requests, reviewing, or discussing — is expected to help keep it a welcoming, harassment-free space,
regardless of experience level, background, identity, or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and be patient with newcomers.
- Keep discussion on-topic and technical.
- Respect the project's documented invariants and decisions; propose changes with reasoning rather
  than dismissiveness.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or deliberately derailing threads.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — the issue tracker, pull requests, code review, and any other
official channel — and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the project maintainer via GitHub (private vulnerability reports are
for security only; use a direct message or a private issue for conduct concerns). Reports will be
reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or ban
participants who violate this code.

This is a small volunteer project; the spirit of the rule matters more than the letter. Be kind.
